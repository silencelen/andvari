The mark

the Younger Futhark rune ár, the "a" of andvari, carved and gilded: two bands with slanted ends, lit from the upper left. Gilded from 128 px up; flat and one color from 64 px down; redrawn on the pixel grid at 16, 24 and 32 px.

Lockups

The name is EB Garamond 620, outlined, one color. The line is EB Garamond, upright, 430.

Type

Your passwords, locked on your own devices.

EB Garamond for the name and headlines; Alegreya Sans for everything you read, like this sentence. Both under the SIL Open Font License, on brand surfaces only: the apps keep their own text style.

Surfaces and pictures

Large fields are polished hardwood with carved grooves on the rune's own slope. App screens sit in a plain warm-black phone outline or a plain computer window, on that ground; other sites appear only by name, never with their logos.

Do and don't

Do
  • Use the files; never redraw the rune.
  • Keep clear space of one rune's width around a lockup.
  • Write andvari in lowercase, also at the start of a sentence.
Don't
  • Rotate, mirror, outline or shadow the rune.
  • Set the name in another face or in two colors.
  • Use the name or the mark for a fork or a modified build.
